Unleash the Power: Audi RS e-tron GT

Step into the future of high-performance driving. The RS e-tron GT isn't just an electric car; it's a statement of engineering excellence that combines breathtaking speed with zero emissions.

  • Acceleration: 0-100 km/h in just 3.3 seconds.
  • Power: Up to 646 HP with boost mode.
  • Range: Up to 488 km on a single charge.
  • Charging: 5% to 80% in about 23 minutes with fast charging.

With its low center of gravity, adaptive air suspension, and signature quattro all-wheel drive, every curve becomes a thrill. The interior wraps you in sustainable luxury, featuring Dinamica microfiber and a driver-focused cockpit.

The Audi e-tron GT is a modern-day demonstration of progress. It
simultaneously radiates sportiness, high quality, and comfort. Its
aerodynamic design and ‘gran turismo’ concept are sure to attract
admiring glances. Driving it is a fascinating and thoroughly emotional
experience.
